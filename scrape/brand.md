## Visual language of the old site
- Photos treated with a heavy navy duotone, red triangle in the top-left corner and blue triangle in the bottom-right, thin red line on top and blue line on the bottom, rose in the lower-right corner.
- Buttons: red fill, white Anton caps (inferred from the repeated red on CTAs; Wix computed styles were not exposed pre-hydration).
- Tagline: "Reforming roofs. Reflecting Christ."
