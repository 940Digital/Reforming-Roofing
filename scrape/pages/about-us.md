ABOUT ROOFING REFORMATION

Thank you for taking the time to learn about the Roofing Reformation family! Co-founders Rob Vaughan and Jeremy Morphis launched Roofing Reformation after years of collaborating in their respective fields. Rob, a seasoned real estate broker and listing agent, and Jeremy, an experienced roofer and project manager, joined forces with a vision of providing an unmatched roofing experience. Their combined expertise has led to truly impressive outcomes.

We proudly offer residential and commercial roofing services to those in Denton, McKinney, Frisco, Flower Mound, and the surrounding areas. Whether you need to fix a sudden leak, replace old asphalt shingles, or install a beautiful new roof on your brand-new home, we are a local, veteran-owned roofing company you can trust. We also perform siding, gutter, and skylight services. 

To learn more about us and our services, give us a call at (469) 321-5201. You can also request information or provide details about an upcoming project through our

 online contact form.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

As detailed above, our co-founder Rob Vaughan has found business inspiration in Proverbs 22:1, which says: “A good name is to be chosen rather than great riches, loving favor rather than silver and gold.” Company-wide, we share Rob's inspiration. The principle in this scripture guides all aspects of our business, shaping how we interact with clients, fellow businesses, insurance adjusters, and more. What does this look like in practice? 

​

We strive to:

​

Communicate transparently. Our team keeps clients informed at every step, providing honest assessments of roofing issues and clear explanations of working processes. 

Keep our word. We follow through on commitments, from showing up at scheduled times to completing projects as promised. 

Deliver personalized services. We do not treat clients' or their projects as merely one more job in a long line of others. When you choose us, we will take the time to understand your unique needs and provide solutions that cater to them. 

Be reliable and accountable. We stand by our work, offer a valuable warranty, and are readily available to address any issues after a project is completed. 

Focus on relationships. Our team aims to build lasting relationships with clients by showing how much we value and respect them.
